Services

Personalized dental care for a confident smile.

Explore our comprehensive services, from preventive care and restorative treatments to cosmetic enhancements designed to help you look and feel your best.

Call (203) 762-7300

General & Routine Care

Comprehensive care to maintain your oral health, from regular check-ups and cleanings to preventive treatments that stop problems before they start.

Teeth Cleanings

Professional cleanings to remove plaque and tartar, including deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) to treat gum disease and restore gum health.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the beauty of your smile with a range of treatments designed to improve aesthetics, from whitening to complete smile makeovers.

Teeth Whitening

Safely and effectively brighten your smile with professional-grade whitening treatments that lift stains and restore a brilliant, natural-looking shade.

Veneers

Custom-made, thin shells of porcelain bonded to the front of your teeth to correct chips, gaps, stains, or misalignment for a flawless smile.

Lumineers

An ultra-thin and minimally invasive alternative to traditional veneers, Lumineers provide a beautiful smile transformation with less preparation.

Dentures

Custom-crafted full or partial dentures to replace missing teeth, restoring function, comfort, and a natural-looking smile.

Dental Crowns

A tooth-shaped "cap" that restores a damaged tooth's shape, size, and strength, providing a durable and aesthetically pleasing solution.

Dental Bridges

Bridge the gap left by one or more missing teeth with a fixed restoration that restores your smile's appearance and function.

Dental Implants

The gold standard for tooth replacement, implants provide a permanent, secure, and natural-feeling solution for missing teeth.

Oral Cancer Screening

A vital part of your routine check-up, this screening helps detect early signs of oral cancer, ensuring timely and effective treatment.

Emergency Dental Care

Prompt and compassionate care for dental emergencies, including chipped-tooth repair, to relieve pain and prevent further damage.

Prosthodontic & Restorative Work

Advanced restorative treatments from a prosthodontic specialist to address complex dental issues and fully rehabilitate your smile's function and aesthetics.
